How compound interest is calculated

Compound interest is interest earned on your capital and on the interest already added. snowballFi divides the nominal annual rate by the compounding periods, the same convention as Excel FV and the HP 12C.

FV = P(1 + r/n)^(nt) + PMT x ((1 + r/n)^(nt) - 1) / (r/n)

  • P: initial capital
  • PMT: contribution per period
  • r: nominal annual rate
  • n: periods per year
  • t: years

Time to double

The rule of 72 gives 72 / 6.5 = 11.1 years. The exact formula the app uses gives 10.7 years for a lump sum at 6.5% compounded monthly.

Questions, answered

What is compound interest?

Compound interest is interest calculated on the initial capital plus the interest already accumulated. Each period the earnings are reinvested, so growth accelerates over time.

How does snowballFi calculate it?

It divides the nominal annual rate by the number of compounding periods and applies the future value formula with periodic contributions. This is the convention used by Excel FV and the HP 12C.

Can I include inflation and taxes?

Yes. Inflation shows your real purchasing power. Taxes on gains can use a flat rate or Spain’s progressive savings brackets. Both are advanced adjustments.

What is FIRE and the 4% rule?

FIRE means financial independence, retire early. The 4% rule estimates that a portfolio can support withdrawals of 4% of its value a year. snowballFi finds the year your portfolio reaches your monthly income goal.

Is snowballFi free?

The app is free and shows ads. A one-time purchase removes ads and unlocks the advanced adjustments and indicators. There is no subscription.

Does it calculate mortgages or loans?

No. snowballFi projects the growth of savings and investments. It does not model debt or loan amortization schedules.

Which languages and currencies are supported?

English, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese and Hindi, with EUR, USD, GBP, CHF, BRL, INR, CAD and MXN.
